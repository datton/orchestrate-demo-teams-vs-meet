Which should you choose

There is no single winner. The right tool is usually the one that fits what your people already use.

Choose Microsoft Teams if

  • Your organisation already uses Microsoft 365 and Office documents every day.
  • You want chat, files and meetings in one persistent workspace.
  • Your IT team wants to manage everything through Microsoft's admin tools.

Choose Google Meet if

  • Your organisation uses Google Workspace, Gmail and Google Calendar.
  • You often invite guests who should be able to join from a link without installing software.
  • You want something quick to learn that runs well on modest devices.

If you are not sure

  • Start from the email and calendar you already use; the matching tool will usually integrate best.
  • Try a real meeting with a few colleagues in each tool before committing.
  • Remember that many organisations use both, because they meet with partners who use the other one.
