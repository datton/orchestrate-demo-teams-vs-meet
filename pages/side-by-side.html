Microsoft Teams and Google Meet compared by topic
TopicMicrosoft TeamsGoogle Meet
Ecosystem fitMicrosoft 365Google Workspace
Main purposeChat, meetings and collaboration in one appVideo meetings, with chat in a companion product
Joining a meetingApp or browserBrowser first, plus mobile apps
Chat and collaborationBuilt in: channels, chats, shared filesVia Google Chat, Docs and Drive
AdministrationExtensive controls through Microsoft's admin toolsControls through the Google Admin console
Learning curveSteeper, with more to learnGentler, with fewer options
Cost modelGenerally bundled with Microsoft 365 plansGenerally bundled with Google Workspace plans, with a free option for personal accounts

Notes on the comparison

Both services offer encryption of meeting traffic, scheduled and instant meetings, screen sharing, recordings on suitable plans and live captions. Which features you get depends on your plan and your administrator's settings.
